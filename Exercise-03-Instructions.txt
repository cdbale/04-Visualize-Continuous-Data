Exercise 3: Regional Customer Profiles

Patagonia wants to understand how customer profiles and purchase activity differ across U.S. regions before planning regional marketing messages.

Use `customer_data.csv` and `store_transactions.csv` to prepare one short client report. The files describe the same customers, identified by `customer_id`. Purchase activity means the number of purchases in December 2018 (`dec_2018`), the latest month in these historical transaction data.

Choose one discrete customer characteristic and one continuous characteristic, such as income or credit score. Use `region` as the comparison groups, rather than as the characteristic itself. Decide which analysis operations and chart forms will answer the client's question.

The Client Report

Include:

- A count table and a clearly labeled chart comparing your discrete characteristic across regions.
- A numeric summary describing your continuous characteristic and December 2018 purchase activity in each region.
- A clearly labeled chart comparing the distribution of your continuous characteristic across regions.
- One finding that connects your discrete and continuous evidence to regional messaging, with purchase activity as context, and one limitation of that conclusion.

Submit a Word document rendered from Quarto containing your code, tables, charts, and interpretation. Use the tidyverse and the coding conventions practiced in class. Choose and justify your approach; different defensible analyses are welcome.
